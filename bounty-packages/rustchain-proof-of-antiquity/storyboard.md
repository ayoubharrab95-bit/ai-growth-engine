## Capture checklist
1. Open cited RustChain documents at their current main-branch versions.
2. Record repository URL and commit/ref for every screenshot.
3. Keep browser chrome visible briefly in source shots.
4. Do not display private credentials, wallet secrets, API keys, or personal data.
5. Use original or licensed hardware photography.
6. Record final narration against the final source revision.
7. Export 16:9 at 1080p or higher.
